Procedure 19-04 Front brake pads: replace
Fig. 1 · Front disc brakeX 000  Y 000
8 stepsAbout 90 min
  1. Loosen the lug nuts first

    With the car still on the ground and the parking brake on, turn each lug nut about half a turn. The tire's weight keeps the wheel from spinning.

  2. Lift the car and support it

    Jack at the lift point shown in your manual, then set a jack stand under the frame and lower the car onto it.

    Never work under a car held up only by a jack.
  3. Take the wheel off

    Behind it is a metal disc (the rotor) with a clamp around its edge (the caliper).

  4. Remove the lower slide bolt

    It's on the back of the caliper. Your manual lists the socket size.

  5. Swing the caliper up

    It pivots on the top bolt like a car hood. Don't let it hang by the rubber brake hose.

  6. Swap the pads

    Slide the old pads out, move any metal clips to the new ones, and slide them in.

  7. Push the piston back

    New pads are thicker, so press the caliper piston in slowly with a C-clamp. Watch that the brake fluid doesn't overflow.

  8. Put it back together and test

    Tighten the slide bolt to the torque in your manual, refit the wheel, and pump the brake pedal until it's firm.

    The first few stops can feel soft. Test at low speed.
