## About

**wrench.repair** is an upcoming AI car repair helper. Tell it what's wrong with your car, and it pulls from official service manuals to give you clear, step-by-step repair instructions.

Not everyone has the same experience with cars, so the instructions you get are tailored to your skill level. Beginners get more detail and guidance, while experienced users get a more streamlined walkthrough.

## How It Works

1. **Describe the problem.** Enter your vehicle and what's going wrong.
2. **Tell us your experience level.** Self-report how comfortable you are working on cars.
3. **Get instructions.** The AI searches official service manuals and gives you repair steps matched to your skill level and situation.

## Planned Features

- Instructions sourced from official service manuals
- Guidance adjusted to your self-reported experience level
- Plain-language explanations of what's wrong and how to fix it
- Consideration of your vehicle and other details you provide

## Getting Started

Visit [wrench.repair](https://wrench.repair) to try it out.

## Disclaimer

wrench.repair is a helper, not a substitute for a professional mechanic. Working on a vehicle can be dangerous. If a repair is beyond your skill level or involves safety-critical systems, you are encouraged to take your vehicle to a qualified professional.
